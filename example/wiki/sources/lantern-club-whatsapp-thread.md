# Lantern Club ops thread, April 2026

WhatsApp export from the "lantern club ops" group ([[ana-reyes]], [[dev-okonkwo]], Priya),
covering 2026-04-02 to 2026-04-29. The month that ran the experiment set up in
[[lantern-club-pricing-call]] and settled it.

## What it settles

**Free works, and price was the constraint.** The free Luma page took 140 signups in four
hours and 260 by 15 April, against Deptford's 41 paid. Dev concedes it the same day: "the
free thing is real then. deptford was the price." That retires the open question flagged on
the March call — the Hackney result was not a fluke of the date.

**The logo did not cost them the room.** Priya asked 20 people leaving the 22 April
screening; 18 hadn't noticed the sponsor pre-roll, 2 noticed and didn't care. Dev withdraws
his objection in writing. ⚠️ This is a 20-person exit survey at one event with the logo
deliberately confined to the pre-roll — it tests the weakest version of his concern, not the
strong one. The strong version is in [[filtering-versus-filling]] and is still open.

**Sponsorship renews on its own.** The Northfield contact asked about three more nights
during the screening itself, unprompted, and the £1,200 was agreed by 27 April — venue
covered through July. Ana took all three rather than one at a time: "the certainty is worth
more than the optionality right now."

## What it opens

Dev's 15 April objection is the one thing the month did not resolve: 260 untrackable
signups for a 90-capacity room is a worse planning position than 41 people who paid,
because paying filters. He explicitly separates this from the pricing argument. See
[[the-free-room-fills-itself]], which is the mechanism, and
[[filtering-versus-filling]], which is the cost.

Priya's 11-step runbook (venue, film rights, poster, Luma, sponsor pre-roll, door list,
projector check, three reminders, afterparty, photos, thank-you email) is the first written
process in the project, and it came from neither founder.
