# Lantern Club

Monthly film screenings in London, run by [[ana-reyes]] and [[dev-okonkwo]]. Obscure films,
borrowed rooms, a pub afterwards. Started out of Ana's own budget; as of April 2026 the
venue is paid for by a sponsor and the door is free.

This is the vault's hub page. The Canonical figures table below is the single source of
truth for every Lantern Club number — when a spreadsheet, a deck or another wiki page
disagrees with it, the table wins and the other document is stale.

## The model

Three screenings in the record, and the model changed under them:

**Free, self-funded.** Hackney, January 2026. 94 people into a 60-capacity room, Ana paying
the venue. The best-attended and best-received night they have run, and it lost money by
design.

**Ticketed.** Deptford, March 2026. £8 a head, 41 attendees, £68 profit on nine days of
work. The attempt to make the thing pay for itself, and the reason the model changed again —
see [[stop-charging-attendees]].

**Free, sponsor-funded.** April 2026 onward. Northfield Reels pays £400 a night for a
pre-film logo; the door is free. 112 in the room on 22 April and a three-night renewal
inside a week. The sponsor absorbs the venue cost, the audience is unpriced, and the room
fills. The mechanism is written up in [[the-free-room-fills-itself]].

### Contradictions resolved

- **Ticket price £8 → £0.** Superseded 2026-04-02. Deptford's £8 produced 20% conversion on
  210 page views; free produced 140 signups in four hours.
- **Show-up rate.** Ana assumed 60% on 15 April; the actual was ~43%. ⚠️ The assumption is
  what the room was planned against, and it was 17 points optimistic. One event only.

## Open questions

- **The £400 is unpriced.** It was invented on a call and accepted with no negotiation,
  which usually means it was low. There is now a renewal and an engaged sponsor — leverage
  that did not exist in March.
- **43% show-up on free signups.** At 260 signups for a 90-seat room the overflow was absorbed
  by luck. A 60% night would have turned 156 people away from a 90-seat room.
- **Nobody owns operations.** Nine days per night, a target of two, and an 11-step runbook
  written by Priya, who is not a founder. ⚠️ The most likely thing to quietly not happen.
- **The room-character question is not settled**, whatever the exit survey said. See
  [[filtering-versus-filling]].
