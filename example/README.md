# The worked example

Two fake sources and the eight pages they compile into. Everything here is invented —
Lantern Club, Ana, Dev and Northfield Reels do not exist.

Read it before your first `/ingest` so you can tell whether what you get back is any good.
Delete the whole folder once your own vault has a dozen pages.

## What to look at

**`raw/` → `wiki/sources/`.** Two sources in, two source pages out. Notice the source pages
don't summarise the document top to bottom — they say what it *establishes*, what it
*reveals*, and what it *opens*. A source page that reads like minutes is a failed source
page.

**Eight pages from two sources.** Three people appear in the raw files and only two got a
page: Priya has one line of dialogue and wrote the runbook, which is a mention, not 15
substantive lines. The log says so explicitly. Restraint is part of the schema.

**The abstract pages are the product.** `patterns/the-free-room-fills-itself`,
`decisions/stop-charging-attendees` and `tensions/filtering-versus-filling` state things no
single source says. Nobody in the WhatsApp thread says "a price collapses demand rather than
filtering it" — that comes from putting three events side by side. **If your first ingest
produces only a source page and some people pages, it hasn't worked yet.** Ask for the
pattern and tension pages.

**The ⚠️ flags.** A 20-person exit survey is reported as a 20-person exit survey, not as
proof. A 43% show-up rate against a 60% assumption is flagged as the first cost of a
decision landing. Noticing that evidence is thin is most of the value — a vault that
launders weak evidence into confident prose is worse than no vault.

**The Canonical figures table** on `projects/lantern-club.md`. One row per number, each with a
status (`actual` / `assumption` / `contracted` / `claim`), a date and a source. Superseded
values move to "Contradictions resolved" rather than being quietly deleted. This is what lets
you say a number out loud in chat six months from now and have it land in the right place.

**Theme-driven sections.** Every page is organised by what things mean, not by when they
happened. `## How she argues`, not `## The March call`. The timeline, where there is one,
is a table at the bottom.

## What's deliberately missing

No `companies/northfield-reels.md`. The sponsor is referenced constantly and has no page,
because the sources contain almost nothing about them — a February email and a man who
stayed for the whole film. That's a real judgement call you'll make often. The right move is
to note it on the pages where it appears and write it when the third source arrives.
