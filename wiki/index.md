# Index

Catalog of every wiki page, grouped by type. Regenerate with `/rebuild-index`
(`python3 scripts/rebuild_index.py`). Summaries are hand-written and preserved across
rebuilds — new pages appear with `TODO: summarize`.

Empty for now. Run `/setup`, then `/ingest` your first source and this fills itself.

See `example/wiki/index.md` for what a populated index looks like.
